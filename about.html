Jaweria Umer

FAST-NUCES · Karachi, Pakistan

LearnBuildImprove

I’m a Computer Science student at FAST-NUCES, Karachi, currently in my second year and third semester.

My journey started with learning the basics of programming and discovering how a few lines of code could become something useful. At first, I focused on understanding syntax, logic, and how to solve problems step by step. As I gained confidence, I began applying those ideas in projects instead of only practising isolated examples. Building Hunter Assassin helped me practise programming fundamentals; the Flight Reservation System introduced me to organising code with classes and objects; and Smart Quiz let me explore how HTML, CSS, and JavaScript work together in a website. I also worked on a hardware-based Dice Roller for Digital Logic Design.

Right now, I’m continuing to grow through my Computer Science degree, academic projects, and practical experience. I want to improve my C++ skills, become more confident with data structures and algorithms, write cleaner and more organised code, and keep building web projects that solve clear problems. I also plan to learn through documentation, practice, feedback, collaboration, and gradually more challenging projects. In the future, I’m interested in exploring cybersecurity further and may pursue a master’s degree in that area once I have built a stronger foundation.
